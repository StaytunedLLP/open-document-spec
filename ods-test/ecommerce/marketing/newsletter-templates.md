# Retention Newsletter Templates

## Campaign Goal

Drive repeat purchases by sending monthly skincare tips, product spotlights, and exclusive discounts.

## Target Audience

Active and churned email list subscribers.

## Ad Creative Copy

"Your skin routine deserves an upgrade. This month, learn how Niacinamide works behind the scenes to rebuild your skin barrier. Use code ROUTINE10 for 10% off your next purchase."

## Compliance Notes

Must include an opt-out unsubscribe link in the footer. Do not send to users who unsubscribed.
