# Clay Mask Specification

## Overview

A gentle purifying clay mask to clarify pores without drying out skin.

## Key Ingredients

- French Pink Clay
- Rosewater hydrosol
- Aloe Vera extract

## Application Instructions

1. Smooth an even layer over clean skin, avoiding eyes.
2. Leave on for 10-15 minutes.
3. Rinse thoroughly with warm water.
4. Use 1-2 times weekly.

## Safety Warning

Do not let clay mask dry completely to cracking point to preserve skin hydration. Patch test before use.
