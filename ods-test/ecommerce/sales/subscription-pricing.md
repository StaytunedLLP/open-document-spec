# Subscription Pricing Model

## Context

We need to incentivize recurring orders (subscriptions) to increase customer lifetime value (LTV) and stabilize recurring revenue.

## Decision

We will offer a flat **10% discount** on all recurring products purchased via the subscription service.

## Alternatives

- **15% discount**: Rejected because it erodes margin too quickly.
- **5% discount**: Rejected because consumer surveys show it is not attractive enough to drive signups.

## Consequences

- We expect subscription signups to rise by 25%.
- Customer retention cohorts are expected to stabilize.
