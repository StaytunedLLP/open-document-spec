# Skincare Routine FAQ

## Overview

General guidance for customer support agents answering routine-building questions.

## Prerequisites

- Access to product specifications catalog.

## Steps

1. Identify the customer's skin type (Dry, Oily, Combo).
2. Recommend the appropriate product schedule.
3. Share the application instructions link.

## Troubleshooting

- **Customer complains of dry skin** — ensure they are applying the [Glow Serum](../products/glow-serum.md#application-instructions) on damp skin, followed immediately by moisturizer.
- **Questions about wholesale** — direct them to the [Wholesale Discount Policy](../sales/wholesale-discount-policy.md).
