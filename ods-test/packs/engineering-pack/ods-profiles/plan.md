# plan

Profile schema for execution, rollout, and migration plans.

## Goal | Objective | Purpose

High-level summary of what this plan accomplishes and why.

## Proposed Changes | Changes

Explicit list of components, files, and architectural modifications.

## Verification Plan | Verification | Validation

Automated test commands and manual verification procedures to confirm success.

## Timeline | Schedule | Milestones

Phase breakdown and target completion milestones.

## Risks | Risks and Mitigations

Technical, security, or operational risks and their mitigation strategies.
