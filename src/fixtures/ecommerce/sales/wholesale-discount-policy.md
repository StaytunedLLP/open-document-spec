# Wholesale Discount Policy

## Context

B2B wholesale inquiries are increasing. We need a standardized discount model for bulk purchases by retail salons and spas.

## Decision

We will offer a tiered wholesale discount schedule:
- **Orders $500 - $1,999**: 35% discount.
- **Orders $2,000+**: 50% discount.

## Alternatives

- **Flat 40% discount**: Rejected because it doesn't encourage larger bulk ordering.
- **Consignment model**: Rejected because we want to avoid carrying inventory risk on third-party shelves.

## Consequences

- Faster inventory turnaround.
- Standardized onboarding workflow for retail partners.
