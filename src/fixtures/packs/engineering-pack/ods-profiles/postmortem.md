# postmortem

Profile schema for incident postmortems and SRE retrospectives.

## Overview | Summary | Incident Summary

High-level summary of the incident, severity level, and resolution.

## Timeline | Chronology

Minute-by-minute or event-by-event timeline of detection, response, and fix.

## Impact | Customer Impact

Business, operational, and user impact metrics.

## Root Cause | Cause Analysis

Technical root cause analysis explaining why the failure occurred.

## Action Items | Actions | Remediation

Verifiable follow-up tasks to prevent recurrence.
