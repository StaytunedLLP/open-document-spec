# rfc

Profile schema for architectural and technical Request for Comments.

## Overview | Introduction | Summary

Context and motivation behind the RFC proposal.

## Proposal | Design | Detailed Design

Technical design choices, data structures, and interface changes.

## Alternatives | Options Considered

Alternative approaches considered and why they were not chosen.

## Consequences | Impact | Implications

Systemic tradeoffs, operational impact, and maintenance overhead.
