# Paid time off

## Purpose

Define how employees request and use paid time off.

## Scope

Applies to full-time employees in all regions unless a local exception is documented.

## Rules

1. Request PTO at least two weeks in advance when possible.
2. Managers approve or decline in the HR system.
3. Unused PTO follows the regional carry-over policy.

## Exceptions

Emergency leave may skip the advance notice requirement with manager notification within 24 hours.
